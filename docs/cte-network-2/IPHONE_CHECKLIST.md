# Real iPhone Safari checklist (release gate)
Automated WebKit runs emulate an iPhone; this is the human pass on the real device. About 15 minutes.
Use Safari (not the Facebook in-app browser). Do it once in portrait; spot-check landscape on two pages.
Mark each ✅ / ❌ and send failures with a screenshot.

## Shell
1. Home loads with live scores; nothing slides sideways when you swipe left/right.
2. Bottom tab bar: tap each tab. Labels readable; current tab highlighted; nothing hidden behind the home indicator.
3. ☰ menu: opens, search box finds "Strib" → Jacob, closes with ✕ and by tapping a link.
4. Edge-swipe back returns to the previous page; rotating the phone doesn't break the layout.

## League pages
5. Standings: playoff picture and table appear without the page jumping; Overall / By division toggle.
6. Game Day → tap a matchup → matchup detail: both scores fit on screen.
7. Rivalries → Duncan Dynasty Bowl: timeline dates and scores visible.
8. Transactions: defense logos show (e.g. BAL/SF), player photos load.
9. Team page (yours and Carter's): current team names (Carter = "The mahomo’s").
