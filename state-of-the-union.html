Counting everything…

How this is counted

  • Everything is read from the public Sleeper API, following the league back through every season. Only weeks Sleeper has finished scoring are counted.
  • A franchise is a roster spot and never changes. A manager is a person. When a franchise changed hands (Jeremy to Isaiah, Tyler to Carter) the franchise history continues and each manager keeps their own record.
  • Head-to-head and league-median results are shown separately; “overall” adds them, the way Sleeper’s standings do.
  • Points since on a trade is every fantasy point the players a side received have scored on a CTE roster after the deal. A traded pick counts as the player drafted with it. Picks not yet used count zero for now.
  • Luck compares real head-to-head wins with what a team would average playing every opponent every week.
