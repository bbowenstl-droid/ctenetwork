Known limitations

Current bracket placeholders are not playoff qualification. The connector’s “playoffs in progress” label conflicts with the league’s Week 10 and Week 15 playoff-start settings; it is not used.

Pending transfers remain separate. Private offers, chat negotiations and unrecorded commissioner decisions are outside the returned ledger. Draft slot is unknown for future picks. No causal win credit or retrospective market values are invented.
