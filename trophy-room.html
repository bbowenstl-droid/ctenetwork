How the hardware is decided

CTE Champion
Wins the championship game. The commissioner’s recorded champion is the final word.
Toilet Bowl Trophy
Last place in the consolation bracket, read from Sleeper’s bracket and the actual scores.
Last Place Trophy
Last in the final regular-season standings by record, then points for. The holder pays for the year’s trophy engraving.

Trophies are credited to the person who managed the team when the season ended. Season honors are computed from completed Sleeper results; median games count toward records but are not head-to-head games.
