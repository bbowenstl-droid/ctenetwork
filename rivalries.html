ANNUAL CTE RIVALRY SERIES

Some games count more.

Six annual rivalry series. Two regular-season meetings between each pair. The second meeting each season is the official rivalry game — trophies, bragging rights and CTE history on the line.

All-time series records below are calculated from completed CTE matchups and follow the actual owner at the time of each game. Carter's record begins with Carter; Isaiah's begins when he took over in Week 4 of 2025. The rivalry trophy game itself begins in 2026.
